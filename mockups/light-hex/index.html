What the table shows

Sample means per hour below. Every sample's error is already there at noon, where light is 1.0. Remove that albedo offset and the part that changes with the hour stays under 1.52 ΔE for every sample.

Still missing, because the game has no resource for them: 31 cattails, 13 plain bushes, the den, cairn, mound and nest. What still differs on sight is content: no shore foam, no cattails, the repeating 128 px grass patch against the mockup's continuous ground (grass layering, planned separately).
  • Departures found: no stage of the light grade departs. Three things outside it do. The vignette (game only) shifts the frame by mean ΔE 4.76 at noon and 2.58 at night, up to 50 in the corners. The season: the game's atlas is summer-only (the registry's autumn and winter variants are dropped by the bake), while the HUD mockup defaults to autumn; seasons on the map are a deferred feature, #397.
